to me, surge is a community for people with hunger, literal hunger, and a hunger for success. i saw students at sfu carving their own paths into the careers they wanted, without the advantages associated with a target school. surge was an opportunity to support that ambition and bring a stronger sense of community back to campus.

i joined as a marketing coordinator and grew into the director role, reshaping surge’s identity through brand building, creative production, and team leadership. in june 2026, i co-founded the hacker experience portfolio, extending my work from how students discovered surge to how they experienced its events.
